# ADR-003: Customer master is `eb_persons`; CRM profiles reference `person_id`

| Date | Status |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-04 | Proposed ⚑ |

## Context
Two customer masters: Elanban `eb_persons` (golden identity across channels) and CRM `customers`/`leads`/`host_leads`, linked only by last-10-digit phone in `eb_crm_links`.

## Problem
Master prompt §3.3/§5.2 require one authoritative customer identity.

## Options considered
A) `eb_persons` master, CRM references it. B) CRM `customers` master, Elanban references it. C) New third table.

## Decision
A (see `data-ownership.md` §2). Lead and customer stay distinct CRM concepts.

## Consequences
Every channel identity, consent and message history already keys on `eb_persons.id`; CRM gains stable joins; phone stops being a key.

## Security impact
Merges become auditable with lineage; unmerge possible; cross-tenant matching impossible (DB per tenant).

## Performance impact
Joins by integer FK instead of `RIGHT(phone,10)` string expressions (current CRM queries) — faster and index-friendly.

## Migration impact
Expand–contract: add `person_id`, back-fill by canonical phone, report unmatched, switch readers; no auto-creation or merging.

## Rollback considerations
Readers can fall back to phone join until `eb_crm_links` is retired (separate approval).
